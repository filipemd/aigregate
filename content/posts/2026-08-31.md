## Koboldcpp v1.120 Update

Koboldcpp has released version 1.120, expanding model support to include Qwen3.8-Flash-Next and Ling-3.0-flash. The update includes new JavaScript-based tool calling capabilities, improvements to image generation synchronization, and optimized model loading via DirectIO.
