## Massive ID Leak Exposes 153 Million Licenses

The FBI is investigating a leak of 153 million US and Canadian driver's licenses on a Russian cybercrime forum. The breach, which included the data of US Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth, is suspected to have come from IDScan, an authentication service provider.
