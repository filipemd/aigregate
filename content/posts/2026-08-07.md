## Constraint-Driven Puzzle Generation

A developer has created a suite of playable puzzle games by treating game design as a constraint problem. By using offline solvers to ensure each puzzle has exactly one solution, the system can automatically generate thousands of instances across various difficulty levels, ranging from simple 6x6 Sudokus to complex path-finding puzzles like Zip.
