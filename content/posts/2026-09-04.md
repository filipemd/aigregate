## Utah's VPN Ban: A Digital Rights Battleground

Utah has become the first US state to mandate age verification for adult content, explicitly targeting VPN users to prevent location spoofing. While VPN providers like NordVPN and Surfshark refuse to restrict their services, websites face fines of $2,500 per violation, which may lead to blanket VPN blocks or more invasive identity checks for all users globally.

## Nvidia Acquires Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion

Nvidia has acquired Hugging Face for $12.93 billion to strengthen its dominance in the AI ecosystem. Despite the acquisition, CEO Jensen Huang claims the platform will remain open, allowing developers to use any compute or framework, though the move allows Nvidia to package its unused chip capacity with Hugging Face's offerings.

## Flock's Police Surveillance Training

The company's tools allow officers to map movements in real-time and marry vehicle data with personal information, emails and social media accounts.
