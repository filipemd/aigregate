## The Assembly Hall of Shame

A research project has identified the 'slowest' possible x86 instructions by intentionally starving the PCIe root complex. The current champion, 'fxrstor64', can be forced to take 62 seconds to complete a single instruction on an AMD Ryzen 7 5800H, fundamentally challenging the notion of instruction latency as a performance optimization metric.
