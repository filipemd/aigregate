## GCC Bans AI-Generated Code

The GCC steering committee has banned 'legally significant' AI-generated contributions to prevent copyright risks, sparking a heated debate among developers about the enforceability of such rules and the inevitability of AI in software engineering.

- [https://lwn.net/Articles/1086041/](https://lwn.net/Articles/1086041/)

## iPad Transformed into Linux Desktop

A developer has successfully ported GNOME, KDE Plasma, and Wayland to a jailbroken iPad, effectively turning the tablet into a full-fledged Linux desktop. The project was heavily accelerated by using 'looped LLMs' to automate the porting of over 500 packages.

- [https://maxleiter.com/blog/xios](https://maxleiter.com/blog/xios)
- [https://xios.maxleiter.com](https://xios.maxleiter.com)

## AMD Zen 6 Overclocking Coming to Linux

New Linux kernel patches suggest that AMD's Zen 6 desktop and laptop processors will support native overclocking and system management on Linux, potentially bringing 'Ryzen Master' functionality to the platform.

- [https://www.phoronix.com/news/AMD-Zen-6-Client-HSMP-Linux](https://www.phoronix.com/news/AMD-Zen-6-Client-HSMP-Linux)

## Intel Arc Media Acceleration Boost

New patches aim to resolve a long-standing limitation for Intel Arc A-Series GPU users on Linux by enabling critical media acceleration support in the modern Xe driver, allowing users to to switch from the older i915 driver for better performance.

- [https://www.phoronix.com/news/Intel-HuC-DG2-Alchemist-Xe](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Intel-HuC-DG2-Alchemist-Xe)
