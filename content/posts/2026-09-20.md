## Google's Android Lockdown

Android users worldwide will lose the ability to install unverified apps by 2027 as Google mandates that all developers provide government ID and pay fees to avoid being blocked. This move effectively ends anonymous app distribution and creates a high-friction 'deterrence mechanism' for sideloading, threatening the existence of open-source repositories like F-Droid and enabling centralized censorship of software on devices users already own.
